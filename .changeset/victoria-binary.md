Install VictoriaMetrics binaries from the vendor's own release archives: `@homeflare/alchemy/victoria`. `VictoriaBinary` puts one binary — `victoria-metrics`, `victoria-logs`, `victoria-traces`, or a `vmutils` tool such as `vmagent` or `vmalert` — into a directory the stack declares, and installs nothing else. The archive and binary digests are pinned in the kit, copied from the vendor's checksum files, and each download is checked against them twice: the archive before anything is unpacked, then the binary before it is written. A version the catalog does not pin is refused before any download. Asset names are matched exactly, so the `-enterprise` and `-cluster` archives next to them are never picked. Only the declared binary is extracted, and an archive holding any link, `..` or absolute entry is refused whole. The bytes reach the host only through the existing `HostRunner` write. A binary someone already placed is recognised by its SHA-256, never by running it, and is taken over under `--adopt` without a download. It never starts anything: the stack's job puts the binary's `path` in its argv. `HostFile` now shares its whole-file convergence with it, with no change in behaviour.
